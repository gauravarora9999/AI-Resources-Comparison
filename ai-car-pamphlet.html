The Performance-Efficiency Framework
Every AI model has a car equivalent.
Performance = accuracy, benchmark scores, hallucination reliability. Efficiency = token cost, speed, mileage per dollar. A Ferrari burns fuel for lap times. A Prius is built for commuting. Know which one your workload needs.

The 3 Rules of AI Model Selection
Rule 1: Route 80% of traffic to Sonnet 4.6 or Gemini 3.5 Flash (Porsche / Civic). They cover most jobs at a fraction of Ferrari cost.
Rule 2: Gate Opus 4.7 and GPT-5.5 Pro behind explicit cost guardrails. Only deploy for your hardest 10% of tasks.
Rule 3: Never use GPT-5.5 for knowledge work without RAG. 86% hallucination means it sounds confident and wrong — often.
